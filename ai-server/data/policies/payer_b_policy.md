# Payer B Medical Policy: Musculoskeletal & Spine Imaging (Policy ID: POL-B-2026-SPINE)

**Payer Organization**: Payer B Health Partners  
**Effective Date**: February 1, 2026  
**Scope**: Pre-Service Authorization Guidelines for Spine and Joint Diagnostics.

---

## Section 1: Lumbar Spine MRI (CPT-72148)

### Clause 1.1: Scope of Coverage

Unenhanced Lumbar Spine MRI (CPT-72148) is covered when persistent low back pain is accompanied by radiculopathy and non-operative therapy has proven unsuccessful.

### Clause 1.2: Mandatory Step Therapy (Conservative Protocol)

Prior to approval, member must complete at least six (6) continuous weeks of comprehensive conservative management under clinical supervision within 6 months of the request date. Requirements include:

- Supervised active physical therapy (minimum 6 visits documented by PT clinic notes).
- Documented trial of prescription-strength oral anti-inflammatory or neuropathic pain medication.

### Clause 1.3: Red Flag Exceptions (Exempt from Step Therapy)

Pre-authorization is granted without conservative treatment when clinical notes document:

- Cauda equina syndrome with objective saddle sensory deficit.
- Acute or progressive lower extremity motor deficit (strength <= 3/5 on medical exam).
- Documented spinal fracture or suspected neoplasm.

### Clause 1.4: Plain Radiograph Prerequisite

Standing anteroposterior (AP) and lateral lumbar spine X-rays performed within the prior 6 months must accompany the prior authorization submission.

---

## Section 2: Knee MRI Without Contrast (CPT-73721)

### Clause 2.1: Meniscal and Ligamentous Injuries

Authorization requires physical examination indicating mechanical symptoms (catching, locking) or instability tests (positive Lachman or McMurray test), plus four (4) weeks of conservative treatment unless acute traumatic hemarthrosis is documented.

### Clause 2.2: Exclusion Criteria

Knee MRI is not approved in members over 50 years of age with known moderate-to-severe radiographic osteoarthritis in the absence of acute trauma or mechanical locking.

---

## Section 3: Safety, Experimental & Off-Label Criteria

### Clause 3.1: Experimental and Investigational Technologies

Standing/weight-bearing positional MRI, upright MRI, or unapproved AI-only auto-adjudications are deemed experimental and investigational and are not eligible for coverage under this policy.

### Clause 3.2: Medical Necessity Ambiguity & Physician Escalation

When submitted clinical evidence contains contradictory neurological findings or when the clinical diagnosis does not align with standard ICD-10 indications, the request must be escalated to an independent physician reviewer (abstain from automated approval).
