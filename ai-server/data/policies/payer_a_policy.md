# Payer A Clinical Policy Bulletin: Advanced Diagnostic Imaging (Policy ID: POL-A-2026-DIAG)

**Payer Organization**: Payer A Health Plan  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Scope**: Prior Authorization & Medical Necessity Guidelines for Spine, Musculoskeletal, and Cardiac Imaging.

---

## Section 1: Lumbar Spine Magnetic Resonance Imaging (CPT-72148)

### Clause 1.1: Clinical Indication Overview

Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine without contrast (CPT-72148) is considered medically necessary only when conservative medical management has failed or when specific neurologic red flag indications are met.

### Clause 1.2: Conservative Therapy Requirement

Authorization requires documented failure of at least six (6) continuous weeks of provider-directed conservative therapy within the preceding six (6) months. Conservative therapy must include at least one of the following:

- Formal physical therapy notes documenting active exercise rehabilitation.
- Prescribed NSAID or non-opiate analgesic medication regimen.
- Activity modification and chiropractic or home exercise progression supervised by a licensed practitioner.

### Clause 1.3: Neurologic Red Flag Exceptions (Exempt from 6-Week Rule)

Prior authorization is approved immediately without conservative therapy trial if documentation establishes ANY of the following:

- Cauda equina syndrome (bowel/bladder dysfunction, saddle anesthesia).
- Rapidly progressive motor deficit (grade 3/5 or less muscle strength on manual muscle testing).
- Suspicion of spinal infection (fever, elevated inflammatory markers ESR/CRP, recent bacteremia).
- Known active malignancy with new onset unremitting back pain.

### Clause 1.4: Required Evidence Submission Checklist

The ordering provider must submit:

1. Complete clinical examination notes including dermatomal sensory and motor grading.
2. Signed physical therapy completion notes or detailed rehabilitation discharge summary.
3. Relevant prior plain radiograph (X-ray) reports completed within the last 12 months.

## Section 2: Knee Magnetic Resonance Imaging (CPT-73721)

### Clause 2.1: Clinical Indication Overview

MRI of the lower extremity knee joint without contrast (CPT-73721) is medically necessary for evaluating suspected internal derangement, meniscal tear, or ligamentous injury.

### Clause 2.2: Conservative Management Criteria

Except for acute high-energy trauma, authorization requires:

- Documented failure of four (4) weeks of conservative therapy (rest, NSAIDs, physical therapy, or unloader brace).
- Persistent joint line tenderness, effusion, or mechanical symptoms (locking, catching).

### Clause 2.3: Prior Plain Radiographs

Prior standing weight-bearing plain radiographs (minimum 3 views) must be completed prior to MRI request to rule out advanced osteoarthritis (Kellgren-Lawrence grade 4).

### Clause 2.4: Exclusion and Non-Covered Conditions

MRI is not authorized when plain radiographs demonstrate bone-on-bone tri-compartmental osteoarthritis unless acute osteonecrosis or surgical planning for unicompartmental arthroplasty is documented.

## Section 3: Coronary Computed Tomography Angiography (CPT-75574)

### Clause 3.1: Clinical Indication Overview

Coronary CT Angiography (CCTA) (CPT-75574) is indicated for symptomatic patients with low-to-intermediate pre-test probability of obstructive coronary artery disease (CAD).

### Clause 3.2: Objective Baseline Testing Criteria

Authorization requires:

- Documented baseline 12-lead resting electrocardiogram (ECG) interpreted by a cardiologist.
- Low-to-intermediate CAD Consortium risk score or Diamond-Forrester score between 15% and 85%.
- Inconclusive or uninterpretable prior stress test (e.g. stress echocardiogram or treadmill exercise ECG).

### Clause 3.3: Exclusions and Contraindications

CCTA is contraindicated and not authorized for:

- Asymptomatic screening in general population.
- Severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²).
- Extensive known coronary artery calcification (Agatston calcium score > 1000).

## Section 4: General Denial & Appeal Governance

### Clause 4.1: Timely Appeal Filing Window

Standard first-level provider appeals must be received within sixty (60) calendar days of the initial denial notice date.

### Clause 4.2: Peer-to-Peer Review Window

Peer-to-peer discussion between the treating physician and medical director may be scheduled within fourteen (14) calendar days of initial denial.
